# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 13, 2026

## Introduction

Shuowen ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Shuowen mobile application (the "App") handles information.

## Summary

**Shuowen does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.**

The App is an offline dictionary reader. Core features work entirely on your device without an account or backend service.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Account or login information
- Search queries or reading history sent to our servers
- Analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage telemetry
- Location data
- Contacts or other personal data from your device

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store the following data **only on your device**:

- **Favorites** — entries you star for quick access
- **App preferences** — settings configured within the App
- **Bundled dictionary data** — the offline `shuowen.store` database included with the App

This local data is not transmitted to us.

## Network Use

Shuowen does not require network access for browsing, searching, favorites, or reading entry detail.

The App may offer optional links (for example, to GitHub or open-source license pages) that open in your system browser. We do not track those visits.

## Third-Party Content

The App includes dictionary data from [shuowenjiezi/shuowen](https://github.com/shuowenjiezi/shuowen) (Apache 2.0) and Jigmo fonts (CC0). These components are bundled for offline use and are not used to collect personal information.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
